Statistical Formulas

Essential formulas for study and research.

Pearson Product-Moment Correlation

\(r=\frac{n\sum xy-(\sum x)(\sum y)}{\sqrt{\left[n\sum x^2-(\sum x)^2\right]\left[n\sum y^2-(\sum y)^2\right]}}\)

Spearman Rank Correlation

\(\rho=1-\frac{6\sum d^2}{n(n^2-1)}\)

Kendall’s Tau-b

\(\tau_b=\frac{C-D}{\sqrt{(C+D+T_x)(C+D+T_y)}}\)

Goodman–Kruskal’s Gamma

\(\gamma=\frac{C-D}{C+D}\)

Somers’ D

\(D_{Y\mid X}=\frac{C-D}{C-D+T_Y+2D}\;\text{(definition depends on tie convention)}\)

Point-Biserial Correlation

\(r_{pb}=\frac{\bar X_1-\bar X_0}{s_X}\sqrt{pq}\)

Biserial Correlation

\(r_b=r_{pb}\frac{\sqrt{pq}}{y}\)

Phi Coefficient

\(\phi=\frac{ad-bc}{\sqrt{(a+b)(c+d)(a+c)(b+d)}}\)

Polyserial Correlation

\(r_{ps}=\operatorname{Corr}(X,Z)\;\text{estimated from an ordinal latent-variable model}\)

Polychoric Correlation

\(r_{pc}=\hat\rho\;\text{from a bivariate-normal latent-variable likelihood}\)

Partial Correlation

\(r_{XY\cdot Z}=\frac{r_{XY}-r_{XZ}r_{YZ}}{\sqrt{(1-r_{XZ}^2)(1-r_{YZ}^2)}}\)

Multiple Correlation

\(R=\sqrt{1-\frac{SSE}{SST}}\)

Canonical Correlation

\(R_c=\max\operatorname{Corr}(U,V),\quad U=a^\top X,\;V=b^\top Y\)

Kendall’s W

\(W=\frac{12S}{m^2(n^3-n)}\)

Mean

\(\bar x=\frac{\sum x}{n}\)

Sample Variance

\(s^2=\frac{\sum(x-\bar x)^2}{n-1}\)

Sample Standard Deviation

\(s=\sqrt{s^2}\)

Standard Error

\(SE(\bar x)=\frac{s}{\sqrt n}\)

z-score

\(z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma}\)

Binomial PMF

\(P(X=x)=\binom nx p^x(1-p)^{n-x}\)

Poisson PMF

\(P(X=x)=e^{-\lambda}\frac{\lambda^x}{x!}\)

Normal PDF

\(f(x)=\frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}e^{-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}}\)

Confidence Interval

\(\text{estimate}\pm\text{critical value}\times SE\)
