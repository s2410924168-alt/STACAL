Statistics Formulas

Quick revision formulas.

Arithmetic Mean

x̄ = Σx / n

Sample Variance

s² = Σ(x − x̄)²/(n−1)

Standard Deviation

s = √s²

Z-score

z = (x−μ)/σ

Correlation

r = Cov(X,Y)/(σxσy)

Regression

ŷ = a + bx

CV

CV=(s/x̄)×100%

Standard Error

SE=s/√n
