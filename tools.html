Descriptive Statistics

Choose the data type first. Raw data and grouped data are calculated using their appropriate methods.

The raw observations will be converted to a grouped frequency table for grouped graphs.
Enter class intervals such as 10–20 and their frequencies. All statistical measures will be calculated from the grouped data.

🔗 Bivariate Analysis — Auto Analysis

Enter two main variables. You may optionally add one numerical control variable for Partial Correlation. Statistical Hub automatically detects variable types and shows only applicable analyses.

For two-column files, the first two columns are used for bivariate analysis. With 3+ numeric columns, a correlation matrix is also generated.
